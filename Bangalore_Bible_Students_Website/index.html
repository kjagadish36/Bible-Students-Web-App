Our Fellowship

A quiet place where the Word speaks for itself

The Bangalore Bible Students are a fellowship of Christians who share one simple conviction: that the Bible, rightly understood, is a harmonious revelation of a God who is wise, just, loving and all-powerful. We gather not around a denomination or a creed, but around an open Bible.

Our studies are guided by the Studies in the Scriptures, a series that traces God's purposes through the ages — from the Garden of Eden, through the ransom sacrifice of Jesus Christ, to the coming Kingdom in which “all the families of the earth” will be blessed.

We are not a business and have no paid clergy. No collections are ever taken. Every elder, speaker and helper serves freely, out of love for the truth and for the brethren.

What We Believe

The Hope That Unites Us

We believe in one God, the Heavenly Father, and in His Son, Jesus Christ, who gave himself “a ransom for all.” We believe this ransom guarantees an opportunity for every person who has ever lived — the living and the dead — to come to a full knowledge of the truth.


We believe the Church is being called now to walk in the footsteps of Christ, and that in God's due time His Kingdom will be established on earth, bringing restitution, peace and everlasting life to all who obey.

We believe the Bible is its own best interpreter. Rather than relying on tradition, we compare scripture with scripture, letting each verse shed light on the others until the whole picture becomes clear.


Most of all, we believe that God is love — and that His plan, once understood, reveals a Creator far more merciful and wise than we ever imagined.

Our Mission

To Make the Bible Understood

Our purpose is simple: to help every sincere heart see the beauty and harmony of God's Word, and to encourage one another to live by it. We believe that when God's character is rightly understood, faith is strengthened, fear is removed, and hope becomes certain.

Your First Visit

Questions You May Have

What happens at a Sunday meeting?

We begin with hymns and prayer, followed by a Bible study or discourse. There is time for questions and fellowship afterwards.

Do I need to be a member to attend?

No. There is no membership roll and no registration. Everyone is welcome to come, listen and take part as much or as little as they wish.

Is there any cost?

Never. Our meetings, sermons and books are completely free.

Which languages are used?

Meetings are conducted primarily in English, and our resources are available in English, Kannada, Tamil and Hindi.

Can I study with someone one-to-one?

Yes. Write to us through the Contact page and a brother or sister will gladly study the Bible with you.

Everyone Is Welcome

Come as you are

Whether you have studied the Bible for decades or are opening it for the first time, you will find a warm welcome here. Our meetings are open, relaxed and conversational — questions are always encouraged.
